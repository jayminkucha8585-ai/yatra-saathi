Yatra Saathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026 (v3)

Yatra Saathi is a travel app for places across India made by Kucha Jayminbhai Nagjibhai, a student developer. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address and (if you use Google sign-in) your Google profile photo. Used to sign you in.
  • Traveler profile (optional): the details you type, and your area rounded to about 10 km, shown to other travelers so you can find travel buddies. You can hide it at any time.
  • Creator posts (optional): photos, captions, video links and the place you tag. These are public to other app users.
  • Group trip requests (optional): group size, dates, places and phone number, so we can call you with bus quotes.
  • Live location sharing (optional): when you turn it on, your name and current location are saved and updated every 30 seconds while the app is open. Only people who have your share link can see it. It stops by itself at the time you choose, or when you tap Stop, and the location is then deleted.
  • Traveler tips (optional): the tip you write, the place and your name. Tips are public to other app users, and you can delete your own tips.
  • Driving licence (optional): name, licence number, type and a photo, used only to verify you.

Information used on your phone

  • Location: used to show you on the map, find nearby stays and petrol pumps, give directions and fill in SOS messages. Your exact location is not stored on our servers unless you turn on live location sharing.
  • Saved items: favourites, trip plans, expense splits, places saved for offline use, creators you follow, emergency contacts, recent searches and settings are kept only on your phone.

Services the app uses

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the online database.
  • OpenStreetMap, Overpass and OSRM: maps, places, hotels and routes. Your search area is sent to get results.
  • Open-Meteo: weather for your area.
  • Google Translate: app text and scripts when you choose a language other than English, and text you type in the translator.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: names of places you search for.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons: places across India, place details, photos and photo spots.

These services receive only what they need to answer each request, under their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not share your licence details or phone number with other users.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase, where only you can change your own records.

Delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile › Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account, traveler profile, creator posts, traveler tips, live location, group requests and licence details.

Without the app: write to the developer at the contact email shown on the Yatra Saathi Google Play page, from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The app is meant for travelers aged 13 and above. Children under 13 should use it with a parent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
